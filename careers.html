We are hiring a sales professional to grow our work with steel fabricators.

  
SalesFull timeUS · Remote / Texas

Sales & Business Development Representative

You will introduce Apex to structural steel fabricators, learn what their shops need, and turn those conversations into long-term detailing work.

APPLY NOW

What you will do

  • Build a pipeline of fabricators and erectors, starting with AISC-certified shops in Texas.
  • Call, email and visit shops to learn their backlog, software and pain points.
  • Coordinate bids with our project managers and follow every quote through to award.
  • Represent Apex at regional fabricator meetings and trade shows.
  • Keep the CRM current and report weekly on activity and pipeline.

What we are looking for

  • 2+ years in B2B sales, ideally in construction, steel, engineering services or building products.
  • Comfort with cold outreach and a steady follow-up habit.
  • Ability to read structural drawings, or eagerness to learn quickly.
  • Clear writing and a professional phone manner.
  • Self-direction in a small, fast-moving team.
