Business Development Manager

You’ll grow Apex’s client base among structural steel fabricators, starting in Texas. You’ll reach out to fabricators, understand what they need, introduce our detailing and connection design services, and work with our project managers to turn conversations into projects.

Apply now

What you’ll do

  • Identify and reach out to steel fabricators, estimators, and project managers, starting with the Texas market.
  • Explain Apex’s services and value in terms fabricators care about: accuracy, turnaround, responsiveness, and checking.
  • Qualify opportunities and coordinate quotes with our project management team.
  • Ask each fabricator what frustrates them about their current detailer, and bring that back to our project managers so we can address it.
  • Build long-term relationships that lead to repeat work.
  • Keep accurate records of outreach and opportunities in our CRM.
  • Represent Apex professionally by phone, email, and at industry events.

What we’re looking for

  • Experience in sales or business development, ideally in steel, construction, or a related technical field.
  • Comfort talking with fabricators, estimators, and project managers.
  • Clear, confident communication by phone and email.
  • Self-motivated and organized; able to manage your own pipeline.
  • Familiarity with structural steel, detailing, or fabrication is a strong plus.
  • Comfort explaining clearly how Apex works with its production teams and who a fabricator will deal with day to day.

Nice to have

  • An existing network among steel fabricators or general contractors.
  • Understanding of the steel detailing process and terminology.

Project Manager

You’ll run steel detailing projects from our Dallas office. You’ll work with fabricators, engineers, and architects to meet project requirements, and keep schedule, budget, and quality on track.

Apply now

What you’ll do

  • Coordinate with fabricators, engineers, and architects to meet project requirements.
  • Run several projects at once with a team of steel detailers, or detail hands-on yourself, keeping schedule, budget, and quality on track.
  • Manage RFIs and change orders.
  • Check drawings to make sure they follow design requirements, Apex standards, and each client’s specific requirements.
  • Prepare drawing packages and issue them to the client on the scheduled delivery dates.

What we’re looking for

  • 5+ years of steel detailing experience.
  • 3+ years of experience using Tekla Structures.
  • Experience in structural and miscellaneous steel construction.
  • Proven project management and coordination skills.
  • Excellent verbal and written communication.
