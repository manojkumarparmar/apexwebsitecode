Design Types

Our services

Structural Steel Detailing

Full 3D modeling plus shop and erection drawing production for structural steel packages in Tekla, SDS/2 or Advance Steel, according to client preference.

Connection Design

Delegated bolted and welded connection design where that engineering scope is assigned, with licensed engineering support for connection calculations.

Miscellaneous Steel

Stairs, railings, ladders, platforms and other miscellaneous metal packages that accompany structural steel scopes.

BIM Coordination

3D model delivery and coordination for clash detection with MEP, concrete, precast and other trades.

Checking & Quality Control

Independent checking of models for accuracy and code compliance, with check prints retained through erection and available on request.

Machine File Compatibility

Sample data files provided for compatibility testing, with output customized to your CNC equipment requirements.

Project Phases

Precision from model to shop floor

Bring us in at bid time or mid-project. Here is what we deliver in each phase.

  • Bid and estimating: tonnage takeoffs, piece counts and connection assumptions.
  • Pre-detailing: contract document review, RFI log, sequence plan and shop standards.
  • Modeling and connections: full 3D model, delegated connection design and coordination models.
  • Approval drawings: checked shop and erection drawings for engineer of record review.
  • Revisions: approval comments and design changes returned with clouded revisions.
  • Fabrication and erection: released drawings, DSTV/NC1 and DXF files, BOMs and field bolt lists.
Frequently Asked Questions

Fabricator FAQ

What software do you use?

Tekla Structures, SDS/2 and Advance Steel for detailing; Bluebeam for PDF review, markups and RFIs; Microsoft Office for documentation.

Do you provide machine files for specific CNC equipment?

Yes. We supply sample data files up front for compatibility testing and customize output to your equipment.

Do you have independent checkers?

Yes. An independent checker reviews models for accuracy and code compliance, and check prints are kept through erection.
