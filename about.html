About us

Steel detailing you can actually reach

Apex Steel Designs is a structural steel detailing company based in Dallas, Texas, serving fabricators across the US and Canada. We built it for fabricators who are tired of chasing answers: you get a project manager who knows your job and a team that is easy to reach.

Who we are

A Dallas company with detailing experience since 2008

Apex Steel Designs works from 1411 Slocum St in Dallas, Texas. Our project managers are based here, and we serve steel fabricators across the US and Canada. We built the company around one idea: fabricators deserve a detailing partner who is accurate, fast, and easy to talk to.

Our approach draws on detailing experience that goes back to 2008, including work with our sister company, AFF Design Services. We’ve grown steadily and organically, driven by repeat clients and referrals. Apex Steel Designs is a member of AISC and NISD in the detailer category, and our team exhibits at FABTECH and NASCC: The Steel Conference.

Our mission

To deliver structural steel detailing that meets every code, standard, and project requirement — through skilled people, continuous training, and current software — so our clients build with confidence.

Our vision

To be the detailing partner fabricators across the US and Canada count on first: proactive, dependable, and genuinely easy to work with.
