About Apex

About Us

A steel detailing partner built around service to the fabricator.

  
Our Vision

Make detailing easier to manage

Our focus is service to the client. Everything we do is meant to make a fabricator's project easier to run.

Responsive

A client-facing project manager stays accountable from kickoff through delivery.

Connected

Models and RFIs stay accessible online so questions move without delay.

Accurate

An independent checker reviews every drawing before release.

Fabricator ready

Machine-file testing and output tailored to your equipment.

Our Mission

Service to the client, built into the workflow

  • Provide clear, fabrication-ready detailing that connects design intent to real-world production.
  • Give fabricators a dependable US-based point of contact throughout the project.
  • Build quality into the workflow through independent checking and documented review.
  • Keep projects moving around the clock through coordinated US-hours management and production coverage.

Our Expertise

What we know well

Detailing platforms

Tekla Structures, SDS/2 and Advance Steel, run to the client's preference.

Codes and standards

AISC 360, the AISC 303 Code of Standard Practice and AWS D1.1 weld symbols.

Connection design

Shear, moment and bracing connections, bolted and welded, sealed by a licensed engineer.

Fabrication data

DSTV/NC1, DXF plate files, KISS and CSV BOMs, tested on your equipment.

Coordination

IFC exports and clash review with MEP, concrete and precast models.

Quality control

Documented check prints kept through erection and available on request.

Global Presence
