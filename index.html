What we do

End-to-end detailing for structural and miscellaneous steel

Plus the engineering and coordination around it.

  • Structural steel detailing — shop and erection drawings in Tekla or SDS/2, with CNC, DSTV, DXF, and other file formats your shop uses.
  • Connection design — in-house, PE- and SE-stamped.
  • Miscellaneous steel — stairs, rails, platforms, and metals.
  • Joist and deck detailing — to SJI and SDI standards.
  • Estimating & BIM — bid models, take-offs, clash detection, scan-to-BIM.
  • Checking & QC — independent, model and paper.

How we keep quality high

Checked in the model and on paper

Every model and drawing is reviewed by an independent checker — in the model and on paper. We keep check prints until the project is erected and make them available on request.

Our work follows AISC, NISD, SJI, AWS, IBC, and OSHA standards and your shop standards, captured at kick-off. Our quality stays consistent from one project to the next, and we are committed to keeping it high. Connection design is done in-house, with stamped and sealed calculations by engineers registered in all 50 states.
