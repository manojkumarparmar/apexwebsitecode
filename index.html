What we do

End-to-end detailing for structural and miscellaneous steel

Plus the engineering and coordination around it.

  • Structural steel detailing — shop and erection drawings, CNC files, in Tekla or SDS/2.
  • Connection design — in-house, PE-stamped.
  • Miscellaneous steel — stairs, rails, platforms, and metals.
  • Joist and deck detailing — to SJI and SDI standards.
  • Estimating & BIM — bid models, take-offs, clash detection, scan-to-BIM.
  • Checking & QC — independent, model and paper.
