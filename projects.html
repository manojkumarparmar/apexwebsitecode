What you get

What we deliver on every project

Every project comes with the same core package, so you know what lands in your shop. When they are in your scope, we add joist and deck drawings, miscellaneous steel, and stamped connection calculations.

01

Job set-up drawings

Typical connections and standards laid out before detailing starts, so you can adjust them to your shop and equipment.

02

Advance bill of materials

An early material list so you can order steel before the final drawings are done.

03

Anchor bolt setting plans and details

Locations and details for the anchor bolts, ready for the crew that sets them.

04

Erection and shop drawings

Checked drawings for your field crew and your shop floor.

05

Single-part drawings

One drawing for each piece, ready for fabrication.

06

IFC model

An open-format model for coordination with other trades.

07

CNC, DXF, and Fabtrol-ready files

Data files your machines and shop software can read. We send samples up front so you can test them.

08

Field and shop bolt reports

Bolt counts, sizes, and locations for purchasing and your field crew.
