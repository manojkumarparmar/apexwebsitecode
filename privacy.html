Privacy

Privacy policy

How we handle the information and drawings you send us through this website. Last updated: October 13, 2026.

What we collect

  • Contact forms: the details you type in, such as your name, company, role, email, phone, project location, services needed, and your message.
  • Consultation uploads: any drawings, models, or other files you attach.
  • Basic technical data: the country your request came from. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server logs.

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics.

How we use it

  • To answer your question, prepare your consultation or quote, and follow up with you about it.
  • To deliver the detailing or engineering work if you hire us.

We do not sell your information. We use your drawings only for your consultation, quote, or project.

Where it is stored

  • Form entries are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts this website.
  • Uploaded files are stored in our company Google Drive.
  • Our project team, including our production team in India, the Philippines, and Singapore, may access your drawings to prepare your consultation, quote, or project.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it for your inquiry, quote, or project, and for our normal business records.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information you sent us, email contact@apexsteeldesigns.com and we will help.
